About

A new consulting practice for AI, technology, product and management work.

Orqveta works across AI, information technology, product management and management consulting. The four are separate disciplines, and most real projects need more than one of them at once.

We take on work where a decision has to turn into something that runs, such as a system rolled out, a product shipped or a process that people follow.

How we work

Start from the decision

Every engagement begins with what has to be decided or changed, and who has to live with it afterward.

Agree the guardrails first

Before anything is automated, we settle what must never happen, what gets checked, and when the work stops and asks a person.

Keep people in the loop where it matters

Routine work can run on its own. Anything unusual, sensitive or client-facing gets a human review.

Leave it running

We document what we build and hand it over in a state your team can operate.
