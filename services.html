Services

Four practices. Most engagements draw on more than one.

Artificial intelligence

Putting AI to work where it earns its place.

We help teams choose the use cases worth doing, design the workflows and guardrails around them, and take them from pilot to daily use.

Typical work

  • Use-case selection and business case
  • Agent and workflow design, with human review where it matters
  • Model, vendor and tooling choices
  • Pilots, and the path from pilot to production
  • Operating procedures, monitoring and controls

Information technology

Systems a business can rely on.

We plan and lead technology work from the first architecture decision to go-live. That covers choosing platforms, running the program, and getting data and people across safely.

Typical work

  • Architecture and platform selection
  • Enterprise application programs, from selection to rollout
  • Data migration and integration
  • Vendor evaluation and management
  • Cutover planning and go-live support

Product management

Deciding what to build, then shipping it.

We work with product and engineering teams to find the right problem, set a roadmap people can commit to, and carry it through release.

Typical work

  • Discovery and user research
  • Roadmaps and prioritization
  • Requirements and specifications
  • Release planning and launch
  • Product metrics and reviews

Management consulting

Strategy and operations work that ends in a working change.

We help leaders set direction, design how the organization should run, and manage the program that gets it there.

Typical work

  • Operating model and process design
  • Program and portfolio management
  • Business cases and investment planning
  • Governance and executive reporting
  • Back-office operations, including finance and billing
